# Early Access

Early Access is the first public claim window for people who joined the Zcash Names waitlist and completed a reservation.

It is not open registration. It is an ordered rollout: waitlisted participants receive access codes in queue order, claim in waves, and help prove the system under real demand before the namespace opens more broadly.

## When it starts

Early Access is scheduled to begin on **{{EARLY_ACCESS_LABEL}}**.

When that moment arrives:

- Reservations close
- Access codes begin going out in queue order
- Eligible participants can claim during their Early Access window

The public countdown on the site uses this same schedule, so the clock and the article stay aligned.

## What a reservation actually means

A reservation is not a purchase of the name today.

It is an on-chain commitment that:

- Gives you the option to buy that name during Early Access
- Makes waitlist participation harder to spam than email confirmation alone
- Places you in the queue that Early Access codes follow

If your reservation is confirmed, your Early Access Code is sent to the email on your waitlist entry when Early Access begins.

## How queue order works

Queue position is name-specific and can change before Early Access starts.

- **#** is your original waitlist line number
- **Adj#** is your adjusted line after referral-based jumps
- **Rank** compares your adjusted line against everyone else waiting for the same name

Only completed reservation referrals count:

- Every **3** direct referrals who reserve improves your adjusted line by 1
- Every **9** indirect referrals who reserve improves your adjusted line by 1
- Partial thresholds do not apply until the full threshold is reached

When adjusted values tie, the earlier original waitlist line wins.

That is why finishing your own reservation matters, and why inviting people who also reserve can improve your place in line before codes go out.

## What to do before Early Access

1. **Join the waitlist** if you have not already
2. **Verify your email** so you can receive your access code
3. **Complete a reservation** for the name you want
4. **Share your referral link** if you want to improve your adjusted position
5. **Watch the public waitlist view** to understand your line, adjusted line, and rank

If you already reserved, keep the email on your waitlist entry reachable. Access codes are delivered there.

## What happens during Early Access

During Early Access, waitlisted users claim names in ordered waves. Referral activity still matters for line position leading into those waves. The period is also a live stress test for infrastructure, support, and claim UX under real load.

After Early Access, open registration is planned for anyone who wants to register and control names without going through the waitlist path.

## What Early Access is not

- It is not a guarantee that every reserved name is already owned
- It is not permanent production pricing by itself
- It is not open registration for the full public

Think of it as the bridge between the waitlist and the open namespace: reserved participants get the first real claim opportunity, in order, with clearer expectations than a free-for-all.
